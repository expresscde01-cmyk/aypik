### Appendix A — Glossary

**My Matches.** Page bringing together the user's interactions (interests received, Matches, archives, and related sections). On this page, profiles are ranked in chronological order of appearance: the most recently added appear at the top left of the grid, while earlier profiles are progressively pushed toward the bottom right, following the usual reading order (left to right, then top to bottom).

**Like.** A simple action allowing you to discreetly express interest in another member's profile.

**Flash.** A priority action, stronger than a simple Like, sending a clear and direct signal to the targeted person to indicate immediate interest.

**Match.** A two-way relationship established between two members, confirming that mutual interest has been established (whether from crossed Likes or an accepted Flash). It is this mutual confirmation that opens access to messaging.

**Messaging and Consent.** As a safety measure and to prevent unsolicited contact, sending messages is strictly conditional on a prior Match. It is therefore not possible to chat with a member without having received, or had accepted in return, their Like/Flash.

**To Review.** The status of a profile whose Like or Flash has been received and has not yet been decided on. The user may then decline the profile, put it on hold, or confirm a Match.

**Put on Hold.** An action allowing you to keep a received profile (Like or Flash) to review later, without declining it or matching immediately. The profile then remains accessible from My Matches, under "Put on hold by you," until the hold is closed: by matching, by a decline, or by automatic expiry after 3 months of inactivity. Archiving is not a closure: it is a personal organizing action, with no effect on the interaction or on the other member. The status remains on hold, the expiry period continues to run, and no notification is sent to the other member. Profiles filed this way remain visible under "Put on hold by you - archived." Conversely, when another member puts the user's Like or Flash on hold, this profile appears for the user under "Put on hold by them," for viewing only: the decision to wait, match, or decline belongs to the other member.

A profile placed in "on hold" status (whether by you or by the other member) without an explicit decision (acceptance or decline) is automatically considered declined after a period of 3 months of inactivity. Archiving a profile on hold does not constitute such a decision and does not stop this period from running. A reminder is sent 7 days before expiry to allow pending requests to be decided on. This automatic closure has the same effects as a manual decline, in both directions, so that no interaction remains indefinitely pending. The decline thus formed, whether manual or automatic, also triggers the temporary hiding from Discover and the Home page suggestions described in Section 3.7.1.

**Matched On.** Label shown when it is the signed-in user who confirms or accepts an incoming request from a third party (a received Like, a received Flash, or a pending interest decided by matching). The date shown is that of this acceptance ("Matched on [date]"). On My Matches, this label may be followed by "— First message" or "— Conversation in progress" depending on the state of messaging.

**Match On.** Label shown when the Match results from the other member accepting the signed-in user's initial request (a Like or Flash sent, then accepted in return). The date shown is that of this acceptance ("Match on [date]"). On My Matches, this label may also be followed by "— First message" or "— Conversation in progress" depending on the state of messaging.

**First Message.** A conversation stays in "First message" status for as long as only one of the two members has started the exchange without a reply from the other party, or for as long as no message has yet been sent. Messaging is open; the exchange is not yet reciprocal.

**Conversation in Progress.** A conversation moves to "Conversation in progress" status only once each of the two members has sent at least one message, marking a reciprocal exchange.

**Archive / Delete.** Management options offered for profiles that have declined a Like or a Flash, as well as for profiles on hold. Archiving keeps the profile in a dedicated section of the My Matches page. For a hold, archiving is only a personal organizing action: it does not close the interaction, does not stop the expiry period from running, and is not notified to the other member (see "Put on Hold"). Deleting removes the profile from this list; for a hold, permanent deletion is equivalent to a decline.

**Ended Matches.** A storage area for Matches archived or ended from a conversation that was already confirmed, sorted by who made the decision. If the user themselves initiated the archiving or the ending, the Match appears under "Matches you ended": the user may then restore the link (and access to messaging) or delete it permanently. If it was the other person who ended the link, the Match appears under "Matches they ended": only permanent deletion is possible, as restoring is not offered. The other member is also no longer shown on Discover or in the Home page suggestions for a period of 1 year (if a message had been exchanged before the ending) or 6 months (otherwise), under the conditions of Section 3.7.1.

**Boost.** A feature that lets you put your profile at the top of the list for a set period of time to maximize its visibility.

**Discover.** The page that lets you browse compatible profiles, according to the matching criteria and filtering described in Section 3.7.1.

**Skip.** An action available on Discover that lets you set aside a profile without sending it a Like or a Flash. That profile is then no longer shown, only to the user who skipped it, for a period of 2 months (Section 3.7.1).

**Suggestions.** Profiles shown on the Home page. They follow the same filtering as Discover (Section 3.7.1) and the visibility modes in Section 3.7.2.

**Visibility.** A single-choice account setting, accessible from the account menu. The four modes are: Normal, Incognito, Stop appearing in Discover and Suggestions, and Pause the account (Section 3.7.2).

**Online.** An indicator (green dot on the photo) showing that a member has recent activity and is not in Incognito mode. See Section 3.8.
